Canon LBP2900

Driver v27.3.3 · LBP2900 / LBP2900B · macOS 27

Install the printer driver and Canon Printer Utility in one package.

Before you begin

Turn on your printer and connect it to your Mac by USB. Finish or cancel any pending print jobs.

This package installs only the LBP2900 profile. Other Canon printers can use the original Canon driver separately.

Installer content is available in English and Vietnamese and follows your macOS language preferences. Canon’s original license and Utility remain in English.
